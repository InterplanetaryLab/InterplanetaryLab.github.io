## Electronics Lab

The electronics lab supports electronics prototyping, limited manufacture, board assembly, and test.

<img src="/_images/elec-lab/elec-lab.png" alt="Electronics lab workstations">

### Work areas and equipment

- Six electronics workstations with overhead lighting, ESD-safe mats, and grounding wrist straps
- Two test workstations with:
  - Rigol DS1104Z oscilloscopes
  - Keithley 2231A-30-3 benchtop DC power supplies
  - Keithley DAQ6510 data acquisition/multimeter systems
- Three assembly stations with soldering irons, hot plates, rework stations, fume extractors, PCB microscopes, hand tools, calipers, and common electronics hardware
- Puhui T-962 infrared PCB reflow oven
- RF test station with two Advantest R3131 spectrum analyzers and an Agilent E4350B solar-array simulator (8 A, 480 W)
- Compressed-gas storage for four cylinders, 4–12 inches in diameter

## Cleanroom

The 100-square-foot cleanroom is dedicated to small clean assemblies. It fits an eight-foot assembly bench where two or three people can work comfortably. Lab protocols govern access, cleaning, and protective garments.

### Capabilities

- ESD-safe assembly table
- Met One 237A particle counter
- Temperature and humidity logging
- HVAC isolation from other rooms
- Clean protocol suitable for light-duty CubeSat assembly

## Thermal Chamber

IPL has two thermal chambers. The primary workhorse is a Thermotron S1.2 Mini-Max, used for controlled hot and cold testing.

### Demonstrated configuration

- Temperature range: −70 °C to +180 °C
- Internal volume: 1.2 ft³, suitable for 1U–3U CubeSats
- GN2 purge
- Thermocouple, power, and custom wire feedthroughs

## Thermal Vacuum Chamber

The thermal-vacuum system is built around an approximately 1 m³ chamber with electrical feedthroughs and provisions for thermal hardware. The achieved values below are separated from upgrade goals so test planning does not assume unfinished capabilities.

### Demonstrated capabilities

- Approximately 1 m³ internal volume
- Lowest recorded pressure: 1.22 × 10<sup>−3</sup> Torr (November 21, 2021)
- Nine total ports
- Electrical feedthroughs:
  - Four 50-pin D-sub connectors, rated to 500 V and 5 A
  - One 25-pin D-sub connector, rated to 500 V and 5 A
  - Four BNC coaxial connections
  - One USB 3.0 connection
- Phase 1A hardware: platen, insulation, heaters, initial shroud, electrical feedthroughs, and computer setup

<img src="/_images/vac_photos/left_side.jpg" width="80%" alt="Left side of the thermal-vacuum chamber">

### Planned upgrades and targets

These are design goals, not currently advertised test capabilities:

- Pressure range from 760 Torr to 1.00 × 10<sup>−5</sup> Torr
- Thermal control from −110 °C to +80 °C
- Phase 1B: GN2 plate, residual-gas analyzer, LN2 feedthroughs, LN2 platen cooling, and LN2 nipple adapters
- Phase 2: full thermal shroud

## Vibration Testing System

The Labworks CubeSat vibration system has an upgraded shaker and supports launch-environment testing for 1U–6U CubeSats.

### Demonstrated configuration

- Maximum random force: 350 lbf RMS
- Maximum sine force: 500 lbf peak
- Maximum shock force: 1,000 lbf peak
- Frequency range: 2–3,000 Hz
- Continuous peak-to-peak displacement: 1 inch
- Peak-to-peak displacement between stops: 1.2 inches
- 1U–6U CubeSat hard-mount fixture
- Configurable for launch-provider vibration specifications

<img src="/_images/shaker_photos/shaker_photo_1.jpg" width="80%" alt="CubeSat vibration table">

### Components and fixtures

The dLW127.153-500 electrodynamic shaker system includes an ET-127 shaker, dPA-153-7 power amplifier, and VL-144x digital sine, random, and shock controller.

## ADCS Testbed

The attitude determination and control system (ADCS) testbed supports calibration and testing of spacecraft sensors and actuators.

### Demonstrated configuration

- One-dimensional uniform magnetic-field generation
- Uniform region: ±0.25 m from center
- Magnetic-field range: ±3 gauss
- Open-loop control
